## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: institutional buyers.** Procurement officers and programme managers at NGOs, donors, municipalities and government agencies who must qualify a water-engineering and water-testing supplier in Cameroon and justify the choice in a file. They usually read on a desktop, compare suppliers, and look for scope, documents, references and contacts they can cite.

**Strong second: phone users in Cameroon.** Engineers, technicians and small-business owners on mid-range Android phones over 3G or 4G, many French-speaking, who want a quote or a water test quickly by call, WhatsApp or form.

Other audiences named by the company: private companies, individuals, students and technicians.

## Product Purpose

The company website for RD Engineering Solution, based in Bonassama-Douala, Cameroon. It presents four service lines (engineering, a water testing laboratory, professional training, products), lets a buyer see what the company can and cannot yet document, and turns interest into a quote request, a water-test request or a call. Success is a qualified enquiry reaching the right desk, from a reader who trusts what they read.

## Positioning

Four service lines from one Douala base, in English and French, working across Africa: engineering and consultancy, a water laboratory, professional training, and products. The site claims nothing beyond what the company already publishes.

## Product Principles

1. Show the gap, never fill it. An unknown fact is a visible, well-designed "to be supplied", not plausible copy.
2. Procurement-readiness before persuasion. A buyer must be able to find scope, documents, contacts and status in seconds.
3. Both languages are first-class. Every page exists in English and French with equal quality.
4. Weight is a feature. A phone's first load stays under 200 KB (Poppins + Brand Display + theme CSS; measured by `deploy/v2/check.php`).
5. Claim only what is documented. Nothing is added to the site that the company has not published or confirmed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA as the standard: contrast, focus visibility, tap targets of at least 44px, one h1 per page, labelled forms.
- English and French equally, including longer French strings.
- Low-bandwidth and mid-range Android phones are the design baseline, not an edge case.
